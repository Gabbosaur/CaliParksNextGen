# Product Requirements Document: CaliParks NextGen

## 1. Project Overview

CaliParks NextGen is a high-tech, smart calisthenics park system designed for outdoor fitness installations. The system transforms physical calisthenics equipment into an interactive "Cyber-Arcade" experience, utilizing sensors to track performance and provide real-time visual feedback.

### Vision

To merge elite physical training with a world-class gaming aesthetic, creating a gamified fitness ecosystem that motivates athletes through data visualization, global competition, and tactical UI feedback.

---

## 2. Target Audience

*   **Athletes & Fitness Enthusiasts:** Individuals looking for data-driven calisthenics training.
*   **Competitive Players:** Users motivated by leaderboards and global rankings.
*   **Park Operators:** Entities managing outdoor fitness spaces who want a modern, safe, and engaging installation.

---

## 3. Core Features & UX Requirements

### 3.1. Kiosk Interface

*   **Hardware:** Fullscreen outdoor kiosks.
*   **Usability:** Touch-friendly with large tap targets; readable from a 2-meter distance.
*   **Safety-First Design:** Rounded corners on all UI elements to mirror the safety-conscious design of physical equipment.

### 3.2. Key Functional Modules

1.  **Welcome & Login:** Cyber-Arcade themed entry point for athletes.
2.  **Challenge Selection:** Interactive selection of training modes (AMRAP, Isometric, etc.) with high-priority icons and orbital animations.
3.  **Live Workout HUD:** Real-time performance tracking (reps, sets, estimated calories) with visual execution feedback (color shifts based on form).
4.  **Session Results:** Animated summary of achievements post-workout.
5.  **Global Network Explorer:** Interactive holographic globe showing active park nodes, athletes, and local leaders worldwide.
6.  **Leaderboards:** Real-time rankings of the top performers across the network.

---

## 4. Technical Specifications

*   **Sensors:** Currently lacks a dedicated BPM sensor.
*   **Analytics:** Calories are estimated based on user-provided profile data and workout intensity.
*   **Dynamic Feedback:** UI elements shift color based on execution form (detected via sensors).

---

## 5. Design System: Cyber-Arcade Kinetic System

The design system is built on a "Cyber-Athletic" aesthetic, prioritizing high-energy movement and tactical clarity.

### Brand Identity

*   **Theme:** Dark Mode by default for high contrast.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary:** Cyan Elettrico (#00f0ff)
    *   **Accent:** Verde Acido (Lime)
    *   **Surface:** Deep dark greys and blacks (#131314)
*   **Typography:** Anybody (Headline/Display), Montserrat (Body/UI).
*   **Shape Language:** Rounded 8px (safety-conscious) combined with glowing neon strokes and scanline textures.
*   **Motion:** Kinetic, orbital animations for active elements; fluid "breathing" transitions to avoid visual fatigue.

---

## 6. Brand Assets

*   **Kinetic Monogram:** Geometric 'C' and 'P' mark representing bars.
*   **Tactical HUD Shield:** A shield-style logo representing data-driven elite "intel".
*   **Digital Athlete Mark:** Iconic front-lever athlete rendered in neon scanlines.

---

## 7. Roadmap & Future Scope

*   Integration of heart rate (BPM) hardware.
*   Expanded exercise library with AI-driven form correction.
*   Social features: Team challenges and direct "duels" between parks.
